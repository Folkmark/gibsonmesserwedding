A note from us

Our Story

Riley and Sam both attended Washington and Lee and even though we were at a school with fewer than two thousand students, we never crossed paths (although we did share a yearbook page). We did not meet until we were both in Washington, DC with a couple of masters degrees under our belts. We sought shelter in whatever restaurants or bars would have us during the early days of the pandemic and we must have put over a hundred miles on our shoes just to spend an extra few minutes together.

Photo

Riley proposed to Sam in our favorite city Charleston, SC with both of our families present. Riley surprised Sam during what was supposed to be a @SweetTeaWithMadi photoshoot in the picturesque Philadelphia Alley. With the church bells ringing, Riley took a knee and popped the question. Luckily, yes was Sam’s answer.

A few of our favorite memories together include traveling to Paris together (and leaving separately), adopting our cat Lars and driving him home from NC, holiday eves at The Deck, and the slow nights at home when we can unwind together. We’re so excited for you to join us as we say “I do” at our wedding in Amsterdam! Tot gauw!

Events

I.

White Out Canal Cruise

Friday, 14 August 2026
5:30 PM – 6:30 PM
Aboard the Wolk · Amsterdam canals

Begin the weekend with a private canal cruise aboard the Wolk—Dutch for "Cloud"—as we drift through Amsterdam’s iconic waterways. Guests will depart from the private jetty of Hotel De L’Europe promptly at 5:30 PM. Over the course of the hour-long cruise, enjoy refreshments on board. We will conclude the journey by disembarking at our welcome party destination, LuminAir, where the celebrations will continue.

Dress code: White Party! Since there’s no bride at this wedding, everyone gets to wear white.

II.

The Welcome

Friday, 14 August 2026
7:00 PM – 9:00 PM
LuminAir · Amsterdam

Continue the celebration immediately following the canal cruise as we gather high above the city at LuminAir for the welcome party. Enjoy sweeping skyline views as our backdrop. There will be dinner, an open bar with wine, beer, and a signature cocktail, and plenty of delicious offerings to carry the night along. The formal festivities will conclude at 9:00 PM, though guests are welcome to remain at the bar and continue the evening at their leisure.

Dress code: White Party! Since there’s no bride at this wedding, everyone gets to wear white.

III.

The Wedding

Saturday, 15 August 2026
5:00 PM – 9:00 PM
Hotel De L’Europe · Amsterdam

Join us for the formal wedding ceremony. Doors open at 5:00 PM, ceremony will begin at 5:30 PM. Afterward, toast the newlyweds in Chapter 1896 at cocktail hour. Once everyone’s had a chance to sip and celebrate, we’ll sit down together for a four-course dinner with a wine pairing. Dinner will last from 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Merriment to follow.

Dress code: For the Boys: black-tie if that is fun for you. For the Ladies: Floor-length or cocktail dress would be appropriate. The grooms will be in tuxedos.

IV.

The After Party

Saturday, 15 August 2026
9:00 PM – Midnight
Freddy’s Bar · Amsterdam

Join us in the historic Freddy’s Bar to Amsterdam, weddings, love, or something else entirely.

Dinner may be over, but the celebration is just getting started! Join us at Freddy’s for the reception as we keep the evening going with great music, great company, and plenty of good cheer. The bar will be open with wine, beer, and our signature cocktail, so grab a drink and raise a glass with us. If the festivities make you hungry again, late-night snacks will make an appearance to keep everyone happily fueled. Come mingle, relax, and celebrate with us as the music plays into the night!

To & from the airport

Trains run eight times per hour between Amsterdam Schiphol airport and Amsterdam Centraal train station. Trains depart directly from the airport. One-way tickets run for about ~€6. It is about a 20 minute walk down the Damrak from the train station to Hotel De L'Europe. Taxis and Ubers are also available.
